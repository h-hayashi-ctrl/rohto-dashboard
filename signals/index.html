What the decisions show

Bug

The agent reads every market as untraded

Both trade logs say "vol 0 SIGS, weight 0". SpaceX actually has — SIGS of volume, the most on the platform. With the real volume the market price would have carried —% weight, and the planned spend falls from — to — SIGS. The direction (buy NO) stays the same.

Cause: market_health looks for volume and deadline keys that the platform's fetch_market_detail evidently names differently.

Bug

Deadlines are missing too

The Hormuz log says market data "reported no deadline". That switches off the rule against opening positions within 6 hours of the close. The agent also gave the missing deadline as one reason for skipping the other markets.

Limits

The daily limit decides which trades happen

On 13 Nov the allocator chose several trades worth 0.495 SIGS, but only one could go out under the limit of — trade per day. — applied markets have no trades at all. The settings end on —.

Forecasts

Both forecasts are judgment, and the agent says so

Neither trade rests on a data series. Hormuz uses a guessed rate of 0.1 transits per 30 days. SpaceX uses a guessed year-end value of $1T–$1.5T, which the agent calls "low-confidence judgment". The strategy skipped markets where it had no grounded forecast, as intended.
